# Ansimuz Legacy Collection

Author: Luis Zuno (Ansimuz)
Source: https://ansimuz.itch.io/gothicvania-patreon-collection
Downloaded: 2026-09-18, free Legacy Collection.zip (20 MB)
License: CC0. Original public-license.pdf included alongside these files.

Unmodified PNG sheets: Terrible Knight (hero), Bridge Heroine (Jev Spellblade), Ogre (Jev Ogre Guard), Grotto Escape 2 Boss Dragon (Jev Ember Dragon).
Environments: Country Platform layers, Gothic Castle background/tileset, Lava Background layers.
Rendering adaptations: scaling with nearest-neighbor sampling, frame selection, mirroring, tinting, and tile composition in Phaser. Original PNG files are unchanged.

These are artist-made assets, not AI-generated images or assets extracted from Dungeons & Dragons.
